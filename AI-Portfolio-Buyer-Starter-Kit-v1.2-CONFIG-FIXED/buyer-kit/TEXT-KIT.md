## Type A — AI automation specialist

### Headline
Automate the repetitive work. Keep humans in control.

### Short introduction
I help small teams design simple AI-supported workflows for recurring tasks—enquiries, internal handoffs, and structured follow-ups—so people review and decide, instead of copying the same steps every day.

### Services (examples)

1. **Workflow review** — Map a repetitive process and identify where AI drafts or sorting can help.  
2. **Enquiry triage setup** — Structure incoming requests and prepare draft replies for human approval.  
3. **Light automation** — Connect forms, inboxes, or tools you already use (scope agreed in writing).  
4. **Handover notes** — Document how the workflow runs so your team can maintain it.

### Benefits (example framing)
- Less time on copy-paste steps  
- Clearer handoff between AI draft and human decision  
- Written scope before any build  

### CTA
Describe your repetitive task

### Short FAQ (examples)
**Do you build fully automatic customer replies?**  
No. Recommended setups keep a human review step before anything is sent.

**Do I need custom software?**  
Often no. Many workflows use existing tools plus clear process design.

## Type B — AI content creator

### Headline
Faster drafts. Your voice stays in charge.

### Short introduction
I help businesses create repeatable content workflows with AI—outlines, first drafts, and variations—while you edit, approve, and publish. The goal is speed on routine text, not generic copy that ignores your brand.

### Services (examples)

1. **Content workflow design** — Define inputs, tone rules, and review steps.  
2. **Draft systems** — Templates for posts, emails, or product blurbs.  
3. **Editing checklist** — A short human review list so quality stays consistent.  
4. **Onboarding pack** — Notes your team can reuse for the next batch of content.

### Benefits (example framing)
- Quicker first drafts  
- Consistent structure across pieces  
- Human approval before publish  

### CTA
Tell me what content you produce every week

### Short FAQ (examples)
**Will AI publish for me?**  
Not in a responsible default setup. You (or your team) approve before publishing.

**Can you guarantee ranking or sales?**  
No. This kit’s example copy does not claim SEO or revenue results.

## Type C — AI consultant

### Headline
Not more AI. The right AI for one clear task.

### Short introduction
I start with your concrete business task, not with a tool. Through a structured written review I assess whether AI is useful, where a human must stay in control, and what a sensible next step looks like—before any larger implementation.

### Services (examples)

1. **AI Check** — Written analysis of one process: benefit, risks, human role, next steps.  
2. **Solution outline** — A practical concept with scope boundaries.  
3. **Pilot support** — Help testing a small version before wider use.  
4. **Team briefing** — Explain the workflow so staff know what AI does and does not do.

### Benefits (example framing)
- Clarity before spending on tools  
- Honest “fit / no fit” assessment  
- Human-in-the-loop by default  

### CTA
Describe your task in writing

### Short FAQ (examples)
**Is a video call required?**  
For a first review, a written description is enough. A call is optional.

**Is the website demo a real AI model?**  
No. The included demo is a local simulation for illustration only.

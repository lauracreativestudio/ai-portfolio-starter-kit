06 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers before you describe your task.

Do I need a call or a Zoom meeting?

No. For the first review, a written description of your task is enough. A conversation is optional and only needed if you want one or if open points are faster to clarify that way.

What is included in the AI Check from €490 net?

A written analysis of your concrete task: current process, possible AI benefit, human role, possible workflows, and data-protection and technical questions. Result: an AI Check report – with no obligation to implement.

Does the AI answer customer enquiries automatically?

No. The principle is human-in-the-loop: AI prepares (analyses, structures, drafts). The human reviews, decides and sends or uses.

Are the prices fixed or only entry figures?

The amounts shown are entry prices (“from … net”). Exact scope and price of implementation depend on requirements, workflow and technical delivery.

Are my data stored in the demo or the form?

In the current version, demo and form run only locally in your browser. No data is sent to a server and nothing is stored. For later live operation, the privacy notice will be updated accordingly.

Who is this offer for?

For small and medium-sized businesses with a concrete, recurring task – for example enquiries, texts or documents – where AI should handle preparatory work and humans keep control.
